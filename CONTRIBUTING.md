# Contributing to BLIND

BLIND is currently a research proposal. Contributions can improve its definitions, test its assumptions, or design reproducible experiments.

## Useful contributions

- Corrections to a specific claim, equation, or trust assumption in RFC-0001.
- Concrete counterexamples to semantic matching or consent handling.
- Leakage analyses and attacks with an explicit adversary model.
- Related work that changes the proposal's design or research position.
- Experiment designs aligned with the [research roadmap](docs/ROADMAP.md).

Open an issue using the research-feedback template. Identify the relevant section, explain the problem, and include evidence or a reproducible example where possible. English and Chinese feedback are welcome.

For document changes, keep pull requests focused and explain what changed and why. Preserve the distinction between proposed behavior, implemented behavior, and verified results. Check relative links, equations, tables, and diagrams in GitHub's Markdown preview.

Use synthetic examples for public reports. Do not include private intentions, contact details, credentials, or third-party personal data.

The complete technical proposal and its references are in [RFC-0001](docs/rfcs/0001-private-semantic-rendezvous.md).
